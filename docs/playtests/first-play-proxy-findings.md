# Coalition, first play: 10 key moments

Played the "Play the demo" path at 390 x 844, through all 20 coach steps ("Finish"), then two rounds alone (rounds 7 and 8). Screenshots are in the same folder.

## 1. The coach says "Do the highlighted step", but the glowing thing is the wrong button (s09.png, s10b.png)
Coach step 4 says, in orange, "Do the highlighted step." The body says "Tap **Confirm Lāyūna**. The demo picked it for you." The only glowing thing on screen was the big green "Hang up" button directly under "The phone". It had the same green glow as the coach's "Next" button. "Confirm Lāyūna" was below the fold, under Duartē's message.
Belief at the time: the green glowing "Hang up" was the highlighted step. I almost tapped it, then scrolled because the text named a different button.

## 2. The pinned coach box covers half the screen and does not scroll (s10.png, s13.png, s17.png)
The coach box stays fixed from about y=320 to y=560. Swiping over it did nothing (s10.png is identical to s09.png). Every phone and move step needed one to five scrolls in the strip below it to reach "Call" or "Try it". In round 2, the visa call row was four screens down, past the radio message and its three answers.
Belief at the time: the screen was frozen, until I touched the lower part.

## 3. Coach asks for a move while I'm still on the result screen (s21.png, s38.png, s47.png, s67.png, s77.png)
Five times, right after a move resolved, the coach changed to the next instruction, e.g. step 10: "Tap **Bīr Amādū Salek about your visa**, then **Call**", and step 13: "Pick **Help Nonā Rosa free the truck**". On screen was my move result ("Success / You rolled 6 and needed 1") with no such row or card. The real path was "See what happened", then "Next round", then the briefing, then "Start round N", and only then the phone or move list.
Belief at the time (first time): I had missed a button. After the second time I learned the loop and stopped looking.

## 4. Message answers are greyed and ignore taps during the demo (s28.png, s29.png, s30.png, s50.png, s52.png)
The top line says "Answer any messages first, then make your one call or tap Hang up". The radio asks "We hear you are on a yacht off the coast. Can you confirm that?" with "Confirm it, from the yacht", "Say you are at home", "Hang up", all greyed. I tapped "Say you are at home"; nothing happened. Holt Greer's "Midnight or never." answers were also greyed. The radio and Holt messages stayed unanswered through the whole demo. Answers only became tappable after "Finish" (s91.png).
Belief at the time: the game was broken or I had to do something else first. I did not know the demo was locking them.

## 5. I opened the phone out of turn; the coach's call was there but would not respond (s47.png to s53.png)
Coach step 15: "Tap **Duartē Albukerkē about your welcome in Esmeralda**, then **Call**." I tapped the bottom "Phone" tab (badge "2"). The phone opened, showed "2 waiting", and the Calls list, but every row was greyed. Tapping "Duartē Albukerkē about your welcome in Esmeralda" did nothing.
Belief at the time: the phone tab is where calls happen, so I could make the call now. I counted this as a STOPPED moment and went back to "Here".

## 6. Police attention numbers disagree with each other (s18.png, s23.png, s24.png, s107.png, s108.png)
Round 1: the bar went from 1/10 to 2/10. The move result card said "Police attention −1". The round summary said "Police attention rose by 2 this round." Round 8: the move result said "Police attention +1"; the summary said "Police attention fell by 1 this round."; the bar stayed at 1/10.
Belief at the time: one of these numbers was wrong; I could not tell which to trust, so I started only watching the bar.

## 7. "Midnight" freight at 3 a.m. (s62.png, s68.png, s73.png)
Holt Greer's message: "Midnight or never." The place line: "Get on the midnight freight." After paying Holt at "Sun 3 a.m.", he says: ""Third car. Midnight. I don't wait."" I believed I had already missed the train. Only on the boarding card did I read "Board between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. with Holt's crate..." The result then said "The train pulls out at 12 past" while the clock read "Sun 4 a.m.".
Belief at the time: I had failed the freight and the demo would go wrong.

## 8. Names and words I could not place when they appeared (s05.png, s21.png, s35.png, s19.png, s77.png)
- "Sosā" appears on the title screen and in the first briefing ("it rises while Sosā searches where you are") with no description. I learned he is a "he" at coach step 2, and that he is "Inspector Sosā", "Interior Ministry, head of the departure inquiry", only in round 7 (s93.png).
- "Vélez's officials" in the first briefing; explained at step 15 as "Rosario Vélez in Esmeralda".
- "the freight" in step 8 ("a card that gets you onto the freight"); explained only when Holt Greer, "brakeman on the Huhūi freight", called in round 3.
- "He can get Atlanta to let you in" (step 10). The destination confirmed in step 4 was "Lāyūna". I never found out what Atlanta was.
- "Your cards lower that number:information 1." and "number:influence 1." on move cards. Read like broken text.
- "the party machine lowers the roll" (step 19), "colônia", "Untested" / "Trusts you".
Belief at the time: these were things I was supposed to already know.

## 9. "Guaranteed success" still rolls a die (s16.png, s18.png)
Coach step 7: "Pick **Decoy convoy**, read the time and the roll it needs". The card showed "Guaranteed success / Takes three hours" and no roll number. The result said "Success / You rolled 6 and needed 1".
Belief at the time: guaranteed meant there would be no roll, and the coach's "roll it needs" referred to something I could not find.

## 10. On my own: did my jet call get wasted? (s95.png, s99.png, s102.png, s107.png)
Round 7: I called Duartē about the jet ("works on the next step, fueled at Susana, and answers in 6 hours"), then used my move on "Pay the fuel agent yourself", which fueled the jet right away. Round 8 phone still showed "Jet: Duartē Albukerkē is working on it / You hear back at Sunday 1 p.m." I then paid the fuel agent a second time; the result text repeated "The fuel truck arrives" and the summary said "Ready to fly at Susana". The visa call says "answers in 10 hours".
Belief at the time: I might have spent a call on a step I then did myself, and I could not tell what Duartē's pending answer would now do. Also, the first round on my own put three messages in front of me at once (radio, Holt, and Sosā labelled "enemy"); Holt's yard gate message was still there after I had crossed the border.
