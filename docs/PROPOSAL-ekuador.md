# Proposal for the kels world layer: Ekuadōr, a second Nelosphere state in South America

*A proposal, not canon. Written by the agent for the owner to ratify, amend or
refuse in quarterback/kels. Nothing here has been written into that repo.*

## Why it is needed

Last Freight Out is set in Valdória and the brief is a midnight freight across
a border. The written world has only one Nelosphere state on the continent
(`world/valdoria-lore.md` defers the rest to the Edeland lore). The owner's
ruling, 2026-10-30: develop the counterfactual that a second South American
country is part of the Nelosphere, Ecuador preferred, "since that's what
happened IRL."

## The real-world analog

Ecuador gave up its own money in 2000 and runs on another country's currency.
That is the mechanism the austral already models ("the austral runs on the
Neloxian peg," `world/valdoria-lore.md`), so Ecuador as a peg state fits the
existing economic logic without a conquest or a company founding.

## Proposed names, by the standing rules

Run through the Spanish-zone crisol phonology of `verdenese/CRISOL-PRINCIPLE.md`
section 2 (c before a, o, u to k; final stress to macron; j to h):

| English | Proposed | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ecuador | **Ekuadōr** | c to k, final stress to macron |
| Quito | **Kitô** | qu to k, unstressed final o to ô |
| Guayaquil | **Guajakīl** | y [j] to j, final stress to macron |
| Ecuadorian | **ekuadōrian** | the Valdórian pattern, one word, all registers |

The learned crust could carry a ceremonial Latin face (*Aequatoria*) on the
Auriverdia pattern if the owner wants one. Not proposed here.

## The open geography question

Valdória's written territory (the Atlantic core, the western corridor to
Litorāl, the Bolivian highland provinces Tariha and Potosī) does not touch
Ecuador. In the game the frēt leaves Salta over the Huhūi passes "on the
Andean line north to Ekuadōr" and the player only needs to be across the line
and out of the ledger's reach. The owner has three ways to close the gap:

1. Leave it. A long Andean rail line through non-Nelosphere ground is ordinary;
   the crossing is the point, the destination is the name on the manifest.
2. Add the Bolivian remainder (La Paz, Cochabamba, Santa Cruz) to Ekuadōr's
   sphere or to Valdória, which makes the passes a true border.
3. Pick Bolivia or Paraguay instead, which the owner also named as options.

## What the game uses today

Only the name Ekuadōr, in three strings (a card preview, Holt's line, the win
text) and the opening narration. Changing the ruling is a four-string edit.
