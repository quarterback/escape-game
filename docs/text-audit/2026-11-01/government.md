## Design inconsistencies

- **Six-sided targets in request previews.** gov.preview.the_aviation_desk_takes_six says "a three or more", but the roll is 8 or higher on the 20-sided die (65%). gov.preview.through_the_embassy_in_baca says "a four or more", but the roll is 11 or higher (50%). The ban preview hard-coded in gov.js:134 says "a 2 or more", but the roll is 4 or higher (85%). See gov.js:208-210 and data.js:497-499. These numbers should be bound from Data.DICE, not typed in (C03).
- **"One more on the die".** The checkpoint order text (data.js:276) uses this phrase, and so do the inquiry's ban text (data.js:219) and brief.gov_job_ban. On the 20-sided die, one six-sided step is 3 or 4 points (sim.js:188-189, data.js:497).
- **"Nothing happens" on failure is not quite true.** gov.preview.cannot_fail says nothing happens, but a failed order still spends its hours (gov.js:203).
- **The preview repeats the meta line.** The order preview repeats time and actor already shown on the meta line, and the code capitalizes {who} mid-sentence ("Done by The provincial patrol.", "Done by You."). It also passes hours as figures (gov.js:125; ui.js:146, ui.js:162).
- **"On failure: {fail}"** is a label-and-value line. A sentence form needs the code to lowercase the fail text (gov.js:125).
- **Costamar's three orders are named as theirs even when blocked.** At Portalba the button says "by Costamaran officials, on your introduction" even when the order is blocked for lack of the introduction. The preview under it still names the provincial patrol (gov.js:119-125).
- **Minister reply.** "Good. Sosā has the address" fires when you name the right stop, but nothing moves; only a wrong answer moves the police (gov.js:171). It is also a position leak (above).
- **"You asked for time"** fires at the minister's deadline whether or not you asked. Ignoring his first message brings it on Saturday at 4 p.m. (gov.js:99).
- **The debrief count is not a count of orders.** It measures one check per hour in which you gave an order or answered a news card (timeline pushes at gov.js:191 and gov.js:255; one per hour at gov.js:294). trail_sum, m_clean and gov.share say "times you gave an order".
