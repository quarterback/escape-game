# Demos, credits, facts and glossary: language pass, Nov. 1, 2026

Scope: every step of both guided runs in `content/coalition/tutorials.json` (title, body, goal), the text fields of `content/coalition/credits.json`, `facts.json` and the notes in `glossary.json`. Each step was traced through `src/tutorial.js`, `src/ui.js` (renderCoach, renderScene, renderPhase) and a replay of both runs against the real Sim and Gov with their scripted dice (the order of play in `tools/tutorial-check.js` and `tools/gov-tutorial-check.js`), dumping the messages, calls, moves, previews and events visible at each step.

Facts used throughout:
- The coach shows only the step counter, title and body (src/ui.js:129-130). The `goal` field is never rendered; it is authoring metadata (no other reference in src/ or tools/build.py). Goals are marked KEEP for that reason.
- Escape demo: Sim runs with `tutorial: true`, which caps police attention at 9 and turns police stops off (src/sim.js:266, 487); `Tutorial.stop()` never clears that flag, and only the government run is released from its script (src/ui.js:771, src/gov.js:351).
- Government demo: the inner Sim run is not in tutorial mode (src/gov.js:26), so police stops can happen there.

## Design inconsistencies

- **The government search step cannot be followed.** Its body tells the player to follow the report and then search, but `done: { orders: 2 }` (content/coalition/tutorials.json:483) is met by Follow alone, because Circulate already counted (src/gov.js:193; gov-tutorial-check trace). The next steps are reading steps that allow no orders (src/tutorial.js:14). Requiring three orders, or allowing only `follow` at this step, would make the original instruction work.
- **The government reports step highlights the wrong card.** At hour 6 a news card is waiting, so `#phasecard` shows "Three sightings at once" (src/ui.js:495, 509). The reports are in `#recap` under "What just happened" (src/ui.js:515-519), and the proves and does-not-prove lines appear only in the move list, which is hidden during News (src/ui.js:507, 630), and on the Route tab.
- **Confirming the travel ban changes nothing.** Circulate already sets the ban on her crossings (src/sim.js:188, 515; src/gov.js:276). Confirm only changes `req.ban` (src/gov.js:210), yet the government demo, the request preview ("A sent message does not close the border; a confirmed one does.", src/gov.js:134) and the Circulate order text (src/data.js:274) all say the border stays open until the post confirms. The escape demo's inquiry_acts step says the opposite.
- **"Ask Costamar to hold her" does not hold her.** It makes her takeoff harder, lets your patrols act at Portalba and allows one questioning in Costamar (src/gov.js:208; src/sim.js:269). The label and the step title stay as they are, pending the owner's decision.
- **The escape demo never releases its script.** "Tap **Finish** to play the rest on your own" and the map step's "at 10 the police stop you" are both untrue for the rest of the run, because `s.tutorial` stays true: police attention is capped at 9, police stops never happen and the eight scripted rolls are used first (src/sim.js:266, 487; src/ui.js:771 releases only the government run; tutorial-check prints "8 dice and 3 enemy actions unused").
- **Front-loaded reading.** The escape demo opens with three reading steps (intro, map, phases) before the first tap, and the government demo opens the same way. The map step teaches the whole police ladder before any police exist on screen. This conflicts with the owner's "no front-loaded reading" rule for the demo. Fixing it means changing the step structure, which is outside a copy pass.
- **The map step highlights `#heat` on phones.** That element is hidden below 1000px (src/style.css:53), and the police attention bar there is `#heat2` in `#expline` (src/style.css:55). The government view step points at `#sosa`, where the report age shows at every width.
- **The rail post roll does not show why it is 14.** The move names only "Easier with the party machine" (src/ui.js:150). The travel ban and the known face each add one to the target (src/sim.js:188, 192), but neither appears on screen, so the step's goal "Read how the ban changes a roll" cannot be met by reading the screen.
- **Messages the demo will not let you answer.** From Pedra Branca on, the Rādiô Portô Venla call waits unanswered for the whole demo. Holt's message joins it in Salta and Sosā's call at Portalba. The phone badge counts them, but no step allows an answer (tutorials.json allow lists; src/ui.js:190, 194).
- **The reception call has no payoff inside the demo.** Duartē's reply is due Sunday 7 a.m., but the scripted crossing happens at 5 a.m. (trace), so the officials the border step describes can never arrive in the guided run.
- **Police or Sosā.** The scene box says "Police are searching…" and "Police act again" (src/ui.js:112-115), while the phase bar ("Sosā"), the route ("Sosā is looking here"), move previews ("Sosā acts once…") and every demo step use his name. The brief asks for "the police" in interface text where it can. The map rewrite introduces him as "Inspector Sosā" once, so the two terms connect.
- **"Residence" with a capital R mid-sentence.** `scene.sosa_at` fills `{place}` with `Data.LEVELS[0].short`, "Residence", so the box reads "Police are searching Residence" (src/ui.js:112). The coach and the `sosa-where` line say "the residence" (src/ui.js:438).
- **Lāyūna may read as a person's name.** Altamira's city (src/data.js:233) was not part of the Oct. 31 renames, and its macron pattern matches the character names (Teresā, Duartē, Nonā). The owner should judge it. The audit proposes no rename.
- **The glossary's hold note says only house arrest ends the run.** A successful climb out of house arrest continues the run (src/data.js:477). The note is internal, but the content check's scope comment rests on it.
- **Inconsistent number style in a government request preview, outside this scope.** "needs a 2 or more" in src/gov.js:134 is a hard-coded string in a .js file. The other previews write "a four or more" and "a three or more".
