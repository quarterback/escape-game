# Proposal for the kels world layer: Esmeralda, a second Nelosphere state in South America

*A proposal, not canon. Written by the agent for the owner to ratify, amend or
refuse in quarterback/kels. Nothing here has been written into that repo.*

## Why it is needed

Last Freight Out is set in Valdória and the brief is a midnight freight across
a border. The written world has only one Nelosphere state on the continent
(`world/valdoria-lore.md` defers the rest to the Edeland lore). The owner's
ruling, 2026-10-30: develop the counterfactual that a second South American
country is part of the Nelosphere, on the ground of real-world Ecuador,
"since that's what happened IRL."

## The real-world analog

Ecuador gave up its own money in 2000 and runs on another country's currency.
That is the mechanism the austral already models ("the austral runs on the
Neloxian peg," `world/valdoria-lore.md`), so a peg state on that ground fits
the existing economic logic without a conquest or a company founding.

## Names

The owner rejected a crisol respelling of the real name as too close to it
and dictated these (owner edicts; apply exactly, never "improve"):

| | Name | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the country | **Esmeralda** | Owner's name. A ship-register shape like *Santa Katarīna*; "emerald," which also sits beside Valdória's gold-and-green. |
| the capital | **Baca** or **Susana** | The owner offered both. The game uses neither yet; one needs to be chosen, and the other could serve as the Pacific port. |
| demonym | *esmeraldan* | Proposed on the Valdórian pattern, one word, all registers. Not dictated. |

No crisol diacritics are added to the dictated names: the owner wrote them
plain, and a respelling is exactly what was refused.

## The open geography question

Valdória's written territory (the Atlantic core, the western corridor to
Litorāl, the Bolivian highland provinces Tariha and Potosī) does not touch
Esmeralda's ground. In the game the frēt leaves Salta over the Huhūi passes
"on the Andean line north to Esmeralda" and the player only needs to be across
the line and out of the ledger's reach. Three ways to close the gap:

1. Leave it. A long Andean rail line through non-Nelosphere ground is ordinary;
   the crossing is the point, the destination is the name on the manifest.
2. Extend one state's ground so the passes are a true border.
3. Move the game's town to Litorāl and make the far side a sea crossing, which
   would change the brief's freight into a steamer.

## What the game uses today

Only the name Esmeralda, in three strings (a card preview, Holt's line, the
win text), the opening narration and the masthead. Changing the ruling is a
five-string edit.
