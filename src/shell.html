        Here is the problem in front of you. Pick an approach to move on. The phone is what your allies are doing elsewhere. Call them to start an arrangement. Each ally rolls a die to deliver, and a promise is not an arrangement until they do.

        Cards are help someone gave you. They lower the die an approach needs. Some approaches need a card. The party card means nothing in Esmeralda. Money works on some people and not on others.
