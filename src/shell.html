      What the inquiry has done

        Each level is the situation in front of you plus everything your allies and enemies are doing elsewhere. The approaches under Here move you on; the phone carries the arrangements that must be ready when you arrive. A promise is not a confirmed arrangement until the person delivers, and each of them rolls a die to deliver.

        Cards are specific help someone gave you. They are not spent by themselves; they lower the die an approach needs, and some approaches cannot be tried without one. The party card means nothing in Esmeralda. Paying works on people who take money and fails on people who do not.

        Exposure is how sure the inquiry is about where you are. It climbs while they are looking in the right place and when someone talks. At 10 they arrive. The run ends at 72 hours. You win when the jet leaves Esmeraldan airspace.

        Coalition

        You lost the presidency of Valdória by four points on Saturday. By noon a court had restricted your departure and asked for your passports. A jet can be found, a country can be asked to receive you, a second document can be issued, a driver can be hired. Each of those is a different person, and none of them can do the others' work. Reach the border at the Huhūi passes, cross into Esmeralda, and leave its airspace in an aircraft bound for Atlanta before the inquiry finds you.
