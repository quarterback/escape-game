How it works. Pick a card. Every person, object or place it can affect lights up in the scene and in the list. Inspect one, then Commit; the action resolves at once and you watch the consequences play out.

Walking between stations takes 30 minutes. Sgt. Osric blocks the barrier until you have Passage; the yard needs stamped Papers or Teo's goodwill; the freight needs a Berth from Brakeman Holt. Heat climbs when people notice you; at 8 the patrol takes you. Wait with a card selected to swap it.
