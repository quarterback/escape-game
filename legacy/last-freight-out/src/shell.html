      The situation

      You are Inēs Biankī, a chart-maker in a rail town in Salta, Valdória. You owe money you cannot pay and the bailiffs have your name. The midnight freight to Esmeralda is the last train before the line closes. Be hidden on it when it leaves and you win. It is 17:00; you have seven hours, in half-hour steps.

      What you need before you can board

      Papers: a forged passport with a crossing stamp. Hire Lūçīā at the market (Commission papers, or Hire courier from anywhere). She takes three half-hours, two if Mārtā's window is open.
      Passage: a way past Gaspār, the customs guard. Bribe him, distract him yourself, send Pipô from the market, or pick the side gate.
      Yard: a way across the rail yard. Your stamped papers get you past Mārtā's window, or make a friend of Theo the foreman (bribe, Sweet talk or a favor).
      Berth: a hiding place on the train. Holt the brakeman takes a bribe once Theo is your friend, or listens to Sweet talk if you prepared a crate yourself with Pick lock.

      How a turn works

      Pick a card in your hand. Everyone and everything it can affect lights up in the picture and in the Targets list. Gray entries say why not. Click a target, read the inspection (what happens, what it costs, how long it takes), then press Commit. Most cards need you to be standing in the same place as the target. Call in a favor and Hire courier work from anywhere.

      Walking between places takes 30 minutes. Pending work (a forgery, a distraction, a lock) keeps running while you do other things. It shows under In progress and as a bubble over the person's head. Waiting with a card selected throws that card away and draws a new one.

      Money, favors, heat

      Australs pay for bribes and hired work; every bribe you pay raises the price of the next by 1. People in town owe you favors. Two cards spend them and nothing earns them back. Heat is how much the town is talking about you. From heat 3 the bailiffs may stop you each half-hour. At heat 8 they take you. Lay low brings it down.

      Keys

      1 to 4 pick a card, arrow keys walk, Enter commits, Esc cancels.

      Last Freight Out

      You are Inēs Biankī, a chart-maker in a rail town in Salta, Valdória. You owe money you cannot pay, and your name is in the bailiffs' ledger. Tonight at midnight the last freight train before the line closes leaves over the mountains for Esmeralda. If you are hidden on it when it pulls out, the debt stays behind.

      How a night works

      You have seven hours, in half-hour steps. Walking from place to place, playing most cards and waiting each cost half an hour.

      Your hand holds four cards: bribes, forgeries, distractions, favors, a lock pick, your own maps to sell. Play a card on a person, object or place to change something in town. The game shows you exactly what will happen before you commit.

      Before you can board you need four things. Forged papers. Passage past the customs guard. A way across the rail yard. A berth from the brakeman. Each has more than one way to get it.

      Heat is how much the town is talking about you. At heat 8 the bailiffs catch you. At midnight the train leaves without you.

      Who are you tonight?

      Same town, same train, different purse. This decides how you will have to play.

      First time here? Play the practice night. It is a guided run on a fixed deck that walks you through every part of the game in about five minutes.
